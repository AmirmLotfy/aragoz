## Locked direction

Carved wood, matte painted metal, cloth and printed-paper placards. Dominant readable orthographic stage surrounded by a dark proscenium. Canvas#F2E6CA, soot#24231F, vermilion#C74632, cobalt#244A9A, ochre#D5A43B. Ochre is accent, not low-contrast body text. Danger adds stripes, shape, movement and sound.

Hero: original compact wooden performer, red cap, chalk costume, cobalt sash, broad hands, short baton, restrained expressive face. Kettle and mirror shells share documented functional sockets. Rigid attachments have their own pivots and obvious break silhouettes. No cinematic DOF, neon/glass dashboards, photoreal metal, hieroglyph shortcuts, horror/gore, glittering noise, or cloth simulation.

Use a self-hosted licensed readable UI family with its license stored before admission; Arabic identity text is properly shaped DOM text with lang/dir, never text baked by generation. Until a font is admitted use system sans-serif. English UI is first; no claim of full Arabic localization.
