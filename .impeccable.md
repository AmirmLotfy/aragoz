## Design Context

Context comes from the creator-provided ARAGOZ brief, accepted implementation plan and approved concept direction, not inferred from current code.

### Users
One desktop Twitch host performs live boss fights while viewers propose/vote through ordinary chat. Solo players rehearse or run a labelled House show. Spectators, including people watching a phone-sized stream, must understand the selected power, flaw and physical module removal.

### Brand Personality
Mischievous, theatrical, tactile. A miniature Egyptian travelling puppet theatre built from carved wood, matte painted metal, stitched cloth and printed paper. Clear comedy and visible causality; restrained motion, never horror, neon/glass or generic dashboard styling.

### Aesthetic Direction
Approved canvas/soot/vermilion/cobalt/ochre palette and original Blender kit; self-hosted Noto with correctly shaped Arabic identity text. Adapt Wayfinder's stage-dominant composition, Playdate's single-family hierarchy and A24's showbill typography without copying branding/palettes. Preserve three physical contract placards and one actual simulation.

### Design Principles
1. Make the power-and-flaw rule and its physical consequence legible before explaining technology.
2. Keep raw proposals backstage and public author credit truthful.
3. Keep critical commands, hazards and health readable at capture/stream size, with non-colour cues and sound-off clarity.
4. Respect keyboard focus, remapping, independent audio/mute, reduced motion/flashes and webcam-safe corners.
5. Preserve the approved art direction; test genuine runtime behavior and label synthetic/provider/human evidence separately.
