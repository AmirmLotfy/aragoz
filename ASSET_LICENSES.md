# Asset provenance and rights

No third-party geometry, textures or audio have been admitted. The source brief and procedural fallback are project-authored. Generated concepts remain concept-only until reviewed. Do not treat cultural reference photographs as reusable assets.

Noto Sans and Noto Sans Arabic variable TTFs were downloaded unmodified from the official Google Fonts repository on26October2026. They are self-hosted in apps/web/public/fonts; no font CDN is contacted at runtime. The upstream SIL OFL1.1 notices and copyrights ship beside the fonts as NotoSans-OFL.txt and NotoSansArabic-OFL.txt. Exact source URLs, byte lengths and SHA256 values are in assets/fonts/provenance.json. The licenses permit software bundling with notices retained: https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/notosans/OFL.txt and https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/notosansarabic/OFL.txt.

Every final asset must record generator/provider and relevant entitlement/license evidence, source revision, concept approval, local authoritative Blender/GLB paths, material/texture provenance, and engine acceptance screenshot. Unknown rights block admission. Keep licensed font/audio notices alongside their files.

Built-in image generation is the selected concept route. Meshy has not been charged by this task. No purchase, trial activation, public upload or external asset publication is authorized by this log.
